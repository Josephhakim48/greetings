How to read the board. Colours identify sessions: green for Asia, pink for London and orange for New York. OPEN means the current time falls within the session's regular schedule. CLOSED cards show the next weekday session. OPENS SOON and CLOSING SOON mean 30 minutes or less.

These are conventional forex session windows, not stock-exchange opening hours or a live broker feed. Asia here means Tokyo; Sydney is not shown. A London–New York overlap is when both of those scheduled sessions are open. Session times are not entry or exit signals.

All start, end and countdown times use Africa/Juba. The small city clocks show each centre's local time. UK and US daylight-saving changes are calculated separately. An end time marked “+1 day” falls on the following Juba date.
